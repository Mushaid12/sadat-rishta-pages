1Our commitment

Sadat Rishta, published by Sadat Foundation, has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Any content, behaviour, or account that sexualises, grooms, exploits, endangers, or abuses a child is prohibited, and we act on it immediately.

2Who can use the app

Sadat Rishta is a community and matchmaking (Rishta) app for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to create a Rishta profile, and the app does not allow a profile with a date of birth under 18. Accounts we find to belong to a minor, or that are used to contact or seek out minors, are removed.

3What is prohibited

  • Sharing, requesting, or linking to any sexual or sexualised content involving a person under 18 (CSAM), including drawn, generated, or edited images.
  • Grooming: building a relationship with a child, or with someone you believe is a child, for sexual purposes.
  • Sextortion, or threatening to share intimate images of anyone.
  • Arranging, promoting, or facilitating a marriage or relationship involving a person under 18.
  • Trafficking, or any commercial sexual exploitation of a child.
  • Any other content or behaviour that sexualises or endangers children.

4How to report a concern

In the app: open the person's Rishta profile and tap "Report", or open your chat with them and tap the flag icon at the top. Choose "Child safety concern", add any details, and submit. Reports are confidential, and the reported person is not told who reported them.

By email: write to mshahintrective@gmail.com with "Child Safety" in the subject line.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police first. In Pakistan you can also call the Madadgar helpline 1098, or report to the FIA Cyber Crime Wing at 1991. Outside Pakistan, you can report CSAM to NCMEC's CyberTipline (report.cybertip.org) or to your national hotline listed at inhope.org.

5How we respond

Child safety reports are reviewed before all other reports, and we aim to review them within 24 hours. When a report is confirmed we:

  • remove the content and permanently ban the account, along with any linked accounts;
  • keep the report and relevant records so we can assist investigators;
  • report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the relevant regional and national authorities, including Pakistan's FIA Cyber Crime Wing, as required by law.

Reports are kept even after the reported account is deleted, so they remain available to law enforcement.

6Preventing abuse

  • Rishta profiles are restricted to adults aged 18 and over.
  • No one can message you unless you accept their message request first, and each conversation has a message limit.
  • Every profile and every chat has a "Report" option.
  • Sensitive documents and photos are private by default and are shown only according to the owner's own visibility settings.

7Compliance

Sadat Rishta complies with applicable child safety laws, including the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act 2016 (Pakistan), and with Google Play's child safety standards policy. We cooperate fully with law enforcement requests related to child safety.

8Child safety contact

Our designated child safety contact can answer questions about our CSAM prevention practices and compliance: mshahintrective@gmail.com.
