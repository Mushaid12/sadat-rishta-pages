1Who we are

Sadat Rishta ("the app", "we", "us") is a community and matchmaking (Rishta) app for the Sadat/Syed community, published by Sadat Foundation. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used and shared, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it. Contact us for any privacy question at mushaidhussain311@gmail.com.

2Information we collect

Account information: full name, email address, father's name, address, gender, language preference, and an optional profile photo.

Rishta profile (optional — only if you create one): display name, date of birth, country, province, city, area, profession, education, organization, experience, father's and grandfather's name, family background, Sadat lineage, firqa, asset description, marital status, number of children, financial status, a profile photo, and up to one additional photo.

Rishta contact & verification information (optional): mobile number, WhatsApp number, address, a contact email, social media links, your CNIC (national ID) number and a photo of your CNIC, family tree availability and photo, a property document photo, and family elder references. This is the most sensitive information the app collects — see "How your information is shared" for exactly who can see it.

Chat messages: the content and timestamps of messages you send through the app's private chat feature, and message-request status.

Push notification token: a device token used only to deliver notifications for new messages and message requests.

We do not collect your precise location — city/province/country are plain text you type yourself — and we do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

3How we use your information

To create and operate your account; to let you build a Rishta profile and be discovered by other members if you choose to; to operate the private chat and message-request features; to send you push notifications about new messages and message requests; to verify Rishta profiles when a verification process is available; and to respond to support requests.

4How your information is shared

With other members: if you set your Rishta profile to "Public", other signed-in members can find it in search and see the profile fields you filled in, your contact details, and your profile/additional photos according to your own photo-visibility choice. Your CNIC, family tree, and property-document photos are private by default and visible only to you — they are only shown to other members if you explicitly turn on "Document Visibility: Community" for your profile. Chat messages are only ever visible to the two participants of that conversation.

With service providers: we use Supabase (database, authentication, and file storage) to run the app's backend, and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging solely to deliver push notifications. These providers process data on our behalf and do not use it for their own purposes.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

5Data retention

Your information is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your Rishta profile at any time while keeping your account, or delete your entire account, at which point everything described below is removed.

6Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time, in either of these ways:

  • In the app: go to Profile → Delete Account, type DELETE to confirm, and submit.
  • From the web, without opening the app: use our account deletion page and sign in with your account email to confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent, not a deactivation: it removes your account, your Rishta profile, your CNIC/family-tree/property-document files, your chats and message requests, and your push-notification registration. It cannot be undone and there is no recovery period.

7Security

Passwords are stored using Supabase Auth's industry-standard hashing — we never see or store your plain-text password. Access to your data is restricted by database- and storage-level access rules keyed to your account, so only you (and, for public-profile fields you choose to share, other signed-in members) can read it.

8Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

9Contact us

For any question about this policy or your data, including deletion requests, contact us at mushaidhussain311@gmail.com.
